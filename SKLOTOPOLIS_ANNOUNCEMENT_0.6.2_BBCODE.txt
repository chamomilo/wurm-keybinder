[center][size=4][color=#d6ad68][b]What's new?[/b][/color][/size][/center]

[size=3][color=#d6ad68][b]Smarter Smart Improve[/b][/color][/size]

Smart Improve can now find the required tool or material inside a sack, backpack, huge tub, or another container placed in a toolbelt slot. Keep your related materials and tools inside and let Keybinder find them for you. C for convenience!

When several inventory items are selected, Smart Improve now starts with the item of the lowest QL.

[size=3][color=#d6ad68][b]Nearby by type fix[/b][/color][/size]

Fixed a bug where a [b]Nearby by type[/b] target could revert to [b]Hovered[/b] when the keybind was saved immediately after selecting an object.
