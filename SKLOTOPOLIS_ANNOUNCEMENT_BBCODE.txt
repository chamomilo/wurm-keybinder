[size=3][color=#d6ad68][b]Hello, SKLOTOPOLIS![/b][/color][/size]

I would like to introduce [b]Keybinder[/b], a new client mod for [b]Wurm Unlimited[/b] that makes keyboard controls easier to create, understand, and manage — all through a Wurm-styled in-game interface.

No more digging through configuration files, building complicated console commands, or trying to remember dozens of keys. Keybinder brings your custom keybinds together in one window, where you can create, edit, disable, and delete them.

[hr]

[center][size=4][color=#d6ad68][b]What can Keybinder do?[/b][/color][/size][/center]

[size=3][color=#d6ad68][b]List of all your keybinds across all alts and all servers. And menu-based keybind creator.[/b][/color][/size]

At last, you can see all your keybinds in convenient list, add or remove, edit in constructor using mouse and keyboard. and NOT A SINGLE ACTION TO DO IN CONSOLE.

No need anymore to be professional linux deveoper to chop your trees in convenient way. C for CONVENIENCE.

[size=3][color=#d6ad68][b]Capture command from your action[/b][/color][/size]

Instead of looking for a command in large list or remember actionID, just push capture button and perform it ! Keybinder will remember it.

Yes, now you can bind "Bash -> stump" and "Smelt this". Or, if you old-school enough, browse vanilla keybind directrory - your choice

All custom commands from server mods are supported.

[size=3][color=#d6ad68][b]Define target for every command[/b][/color][/size]

This one i like the most in Custom Actions mod of Bdew. Each command have flexible target choice. toolbelt slot, equipment slot, selected or hovered item, nearby, tile behind you... you name it. I added some options like Exact object (pre-selec your shovel strignt from inventory or specify that shield as a target for your improve actions), "nearby by type" (select nearby stump from any tree), "your ride" (yes you can target your ride)

[size=3][color=#d6ad68][b]One keybind may be set of command within your action que[/b][/color][/size]

This is also from Custom actions of Bdew. Activate rake, then farm your 7x7, then harvest&replant your 7x7 and name it E key. Cool. And no automations of any sort - all within your character's action queue limit.

[size=3][color=#d6ad68][b]Muli-keybind key[/b][/color][/size]

This is something completely new. several (up to 15!) keybinds can be binded to SAME KEY !!! you can have 15 command behind "E" and 15 behind "Q". switching is plain simple - long press and choose. it wll render opbsolete keybind files forever, and you dont need to be piano player anymore - just enjoy game as a wurm player !

[size=3][color=#d6ad68][b]Mouse wheel binding[/b][/color][/size]

Mouse wheel finally starts working. bind push/pull to it, carefully define target - and you will be greatly satisified with your forge following your hand waving. profit ! Ctrl-wheel for turning = super profit !

and yes, i fixed target de-selection when you make multiple push. push harder and more often.

[size=3][color=#d6ad68][b]Embarking with head turn[/b][/color][/size]

Yes, now you can bind embark/disembark, and your character will turn his head toward horses. finally it happened...

[size=3][color=#d6ad68][b]Improve items with one smart key[/b][/color][/size]

Keybinder includes [b]Smart Improve[/b], an updated reimplementation inspired by the Improved Improve family of mods. Choose an improve key, keep the required tools and materials in your inventory or backpacks, define a target, and Keybinder will select the appropriate resource. The old Improved Improve or i2improve mods are not required; you can delete them, as well as Custom Actions.

[size=3][color=#d6ad68][b]Alts-friendly[/b][/color][/size]

Keybinder works good with alts. All of them work with same keybinder directory. You can filter (or filter out) only keybinds created by this specific alt. Plus, keybinder will remember which keybinds where selected by your enchanter, and auto-enable them. for him or her. Sweet.

Same for servers - you can have one set for inf and another for Nov. And yes, Keybinder is Sklotopolis-friendly and knows about Inf and Nov.

[size=3][color=#d6ad68][b]Server-friendly[/b][/color][/size]

Keybinder will closely monitor your action que and will not run long or short command if you dont have enogh space in it, filled with something. no more server spamming.

[center][size=4][color=#d6ad68][b]Installation[/b][/color][/size][/center]

[b]1.[/b] Disable the old [b]action[/b] and [b]i2improve[/b] client mods.

[b]2.[/b] Download latest Keybinder.zip: [url=https://github.com/chamomilo/wurm-keybinder/releases/tag/v0.6.1][b]Github repository[/b][/url]

[b]3.[/b] Extract [b]keybinder-0.6.1.zip[/b] (or latest one) into your Wurm Unlimited client directory, as usual for Ago's Client Mod Launcher.

[b]4.[/b] Start the game. If the introduction does not appear, enable [b]Keybinder[/b] in [b]HUD Settings[/b].

[b]5.[/b] if you do upgrade, just overwrite, dont worry

[center][b]You do not need to use the console with this mod.
Everything can be configured through the in-game interface.[/b][/center]
